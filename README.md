## OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme takes a customer-first, iterative approach: teams deliver small, testable increments, make data-informed decisions, and maintain clear ownership. Projects move from initiation and stakeholder alignment through planning, execution, release, and retrospective. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, risks, and communication; the Product Manager owns outcomes and priorities; developers and QA collaborate to build and validate the work, with stakeholders providing input and approvals.

Teams use a project board and prioritized backlog to coordinate work, with daily standups, weekly delivery and PM–Product syncs, regular stakeholder updates, and demos at sprint or milestone ends. Risks and dependencies are tracked with owners and mitigations, reviewed regularly, and escalated as needed. Quality practices include explicit acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, peer review, automated CI tests and security scans, appropriate manual QA and smoke tests, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives turn lessons from sprints, releases, and incidents into owned improvement actions.
