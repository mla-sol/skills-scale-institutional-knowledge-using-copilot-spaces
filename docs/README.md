# OctoAcme Project Management

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative approach: teams deliver small, testable increments, make data-informed decisions, and maintain clear ownership. A project starts with a one-page brief that establishes the problem, outcomes, success measures, stakeholders, and initial risks. Once aligned, the team plans prioritized work, acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, and dependencies.

During execution, teams coordinate through a project board, daily standups, weekly delivery and PM–Product syncs, demos, and regular stakeholder updates. The Project Manager coordinates schedules, risks, and communication; the Product Manager owns outcomes and prioritization; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks and dependencies are tracked with owners and mitigations, reviewed regularly, and escalated when needed.

Quality is built into delivery through peer review, automated CI tests and security scans, appropriate integration and manual QA, and smoke tests for critical flows. Before release, teams confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, and rollback plans; after deployment, they verify the release and communicate it. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, and incidents turn lessons into owned, trackable improvements.
